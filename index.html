MACHINE LEARNING RESEARCHER

Hi, I’m Sabbir Ahmed.

I’m an M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Jashore University of Science and Technology, where I also work as a teaching assistant.

My research interests are computer vision, large vision-language models, and multimodal learning. I currently study image mixing and generalization at the Visual Machine Intelligence Lab, and language-model fine-tuning with the Bangladesh Computational Social Science Lab.

I’m looking for PhD opportunities in these areas and would welcome conversations with potential supervisors.

01

IMAGE MIXING & GENERALIZATION

Understanding how image mixing shapes learning

I investigate how intra-class and inter-class mixing affect model performance and learned representations. The study spans 273 experiments on CIFAR/ImageNet, including CutMix, SaliencyMix, MixUp, and PuzzleMix.

With Dr. A F M Shahab Uddin · Working paper

02

LANGUAGE MODELS & COMPUTATIONAL SOCIAL SCIENCE

Studying institutional discourse with language models

I develop classification, fine-tuning, and distributed inference workflows to study DEI discourse across 500 U.S. universities. The work includes 700,000 classified tweets within a 9-million-tweet corpus.

With Dr. Nur Ahmed · Working paper

03

COMPUTER VISION · UNDERGRADUATE THESIS

Guided unsupervised segmentation

My undergraduate thesis explored cross-model prompting for medical image segmentation. My current master’s thesis focuses on robust fine-tuning and alignment of large language models.

Jashore University of Science and Technology
