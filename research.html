EXPERIMENTS & OPEN QUESTIONS

Research in progress

I study how models learn from data and how to build reliable workflows around them.

COMPUTER VISION

How does image mixing change what a model learns?

Mixing images creates new training examples, but the choice of what to mix matters. I investigate intra-class and inter-class dynamics across CutMix, SaliencyMix, MixUp, and PuzzleMix.

My work spans 273 experiments on CIFAR/ImageNet, examining performance, feature representations, and generalization across architectures.

With Dr. A F M Shahab Uddin · Visual Machine Intelligence Lab, JUST

LANGUAGE MODELS

Studying institutional discourse at scale

How can language models help examine DEI discourse across U.S. universities? I develop classification and fine-tuning workflows that connect a large text corpus to this research question.

The work covers 700,000 classified tweets within a 9-million-tweet corpus across 500 universities. My contributions include BERTweet and Qwen fine-tuning, distributed inference, adaptive batching, and fault-tolerant worker coordination.

With Dr. Nur Ahmed · Bangladesh Computational Social Science Lab
